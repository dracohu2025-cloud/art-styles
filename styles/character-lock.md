# Style-first lock

This gallery is **style-first**. Characters are not locked.

- Each still must show **one dominant art style**
- Characters may differ between frames
- Native landscape **16:9** (1672×941) — no crop, no upscale, no strip sheets
- Fully clothed original characters only; no IP lookalikes
- English name primary (`name_en`), Chinese secondary (`name_zh`)

Imports skip anything named REJECT and anything under `strips/`.
